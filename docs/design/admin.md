# Admin pages

A surface section of the design system. Tokens, type, components and Named Rules live in [`DESIGN.md`](../../DESIGN.md); a "see *X*" names another surface in this folder or a section of `DESIGN.md`.

Categories, Suggestions, Categorizers and Members are under Manage, for admins, and only need to work on desktop. Each page reads first: it shows what is set up, and editing is a step you take on purpose. An action that can't run is replaced by a muted 12px reason with a 14px info icon ("You are the only active admin. Make someone else an admin first."), never just greyed out. Deactivate, Make admin, Delete, Revoke and Ask again confirm inline, as Rules does.
- **Categories.** The title row, then a muted line: "Every Transaction gets a Subcategory. Each Category ends with a catch-all, which takes the Transactions and Rules of any Subcategory you delete. A Category keeps its colour wherever you move it."
  - **Cards:** a grid of read-only Category cards (`auto-fill`, at least 320px, 16px gap), in Category order. Each card has a 4px top band in its Category hue. Its head shows the hue dot and name (Title), the kind (Spending or Income, muted 12px 500) and a small ghost "Edit". Below come its Subcategories, 13px Ink 2 rows between hairlines, each with its Transaction count (muted, tabular). The catch-all carries a dashed "catch-all" tag, and the bank-fees Subcategory carries a "bank fees" tag. The last tile is a dashed "New Category" button.
  - **Editing one Category:** Edit opens that card across two columns, with a 2px Household Indigo ring and the Pop shadow. Every other Edit is disabled until it closes. Its head reads "Editing Food", "Spending · place 1 of 10", with ‹ › buttons to move the Category earlier or later. Its name is a 38px field, and each Subcategory row is a 34px name field, "412 Transactions · 4 Rules", and Move up, Move down and a ghost Delete. The catch-all row has no buttons and is tagged "catch-all · last". Under the rows is "New Subcategory, goes above Other" with Add. The foot holds a ghost "Delete Food", which is disabled with its reason ("Food still has 555 Transactions. Delete its Subcategories first, then recategorize what is left in Other."), Cancel, and the primary "Save changes", which saves the names. Moves, adds and deletes apply at once.
  - **Delete a Subcategory:** "Delete Food › Groceries? Its 412 Transactions and 4 Rules move to Food › Other. Queued suggestions for it are asked again." with "Keep it" and "Delete and move".
  - **Delete a Category:** offered only when no Transaction or Rule uses it: "Delete Garden and its catch-all Other? Nothing uses it."
  - **New Category:** a name and a Spending/Income select, with "It starts with one catch-all, Other (Other income for an income Category)". The new card goes last. Its hue follows its id.
- **Suggestions.** The title row, then a muted line: "What no Rule decides waits for a Categorizer, which suggests a Subcategory and how sure it is. A suggestion sure enough categorizes its Transaction by itself; the rest wait in the Review queue with the suggestion first."
  - **Status strip:** one card in a row with four stats (muted 12px label over a 15px 600 tabular figure, with a muted 12px 400 caption after it): "Waiting for a suggestion 3 since 25 Oct 2026 09:30" (when the Statement of the Transaction waiting longest was imported; "0 nothing waits"), "Last suggestion 14:02 from Ollama on the NAS" (the time today, else the date and time, and the token's name; "none yet"), With a suggestion, and By Categorizers this month. A "Categorizers" button with the key icon sits at the right. Pursegraph can't know whether a Categorizer is working, so the strip has no state and no Check now: a long wait with no recent suggestion is the sign that something is off.
  - **How sure the waiting suggestions are:** a bar chart of the queued suggestions in 5 % bins from 0 to 100 %, with counts above the bars and tens labelled under the axis. A 2px Household Indigo line marks the threshold, labelled "Auto-accept · 85 %" in Indigo Ink (chrome, not data). Bars below the line are Line Strong, and bars past it are Ink 2. The legend reads "Below the line: wait for a Member · Past the line: would categorize by itself". The chart has a text `aria-label` listing each non-empty bin.
  - **The threshold:** "Auto-accept at", then a range slider and a number field kept in step, both in 5 % steps from 50 to 100 and in "% sure", the queue's unit. Moving the slider moves the line and recolours the bars live. Save stays disabled until the value changes. Then "Keep 85 %" appears, and the save reads "Save 70 %", or "Save, then review 5" when lowering it lets queued suggestions through. A muted line says "Raising it never undoes what a Categorizer already set. Lowering it offers the queued suggestions past the line."
  - **After lowering:** an Indigo Wash notice: "5 Transactions in the Review queue are now at least 70 % sure. Accept them now, or leave them in the queue with the suggestion first.", with "Leave in the queue" and the primary "Accept 5".
  - **Ask again:** a card with "After a Categorizer changes (a new model or prompt, say), clear the queued suggestions so they're suggested again." and "Ask again about 17 suggestions", which confirms: "Ask the Categorizers again about the 17 suggestions in the Review queue? They're cleared and come back as the Categorizers answer. Outcomes already set stay."
- **Categorizers.** The title row, then a muted line: "A Categorizer is a program, such as one that asks an LLM, that fetches the Transactions no Rule decides and sends back a suggested Subcategory with how sure it is. Each connects with a token of its own, which can do nothing else. Webhooks tell it when there is something to fetch." Four cards, at most 900px wide, 16px apart: Tokens, New token, Webhooks and New Webhook.
  - **Tokens · 2:** a row per active token, between hairlines: the name (500), and under it a muted 12px "created 27 Oct 2026 by Bor · last used 27 Oct 2026 14:02" (or "never used"). A ghost 30px "Revoke" sits at the right and confirms inline under the row: "Revoke **Ollama on the NAS**? Its Categorizer can't connect any more. The suggestions it already sent stay." (Keep it / Revoke). With no token: "No Categorizer can connect yet, so the Review queue fills without suggestions."
  - **New token:** the reason line, with the info icon, says where Transaction text goes: "The Categorizer gets each waiting Transaction's description, amount and details, and the Household's earlier choices as examples. Where it sends them is up to it: possibly outside this network, such as to a cloud service." Then "Name" with a field ("e.g. Ollama on the NAS") and the primary "Create token" with the key icon.
  - **The token:** a dialog like Members' link dialog, titled "Token for Ollama on the NAS": "Give it to the Categorizer, which sends it with each call as `Authorization: Bearer`.", the token in the read-only monospace field with a primary "Copy token", and two facts: "**Shown only now.** Once you close this you can't see it again; if it's lost, revoke it and create another." and "It can only fetch the Transactions waiting for a suggestion and send suggestions back. It can't log in." Done closes it.
  - **Webhooks · 1:** a muted line, "Pursegraph calls each of these when an import, Ask again or a deleted Subcategory leaves Transactions waiting for a suggestion, so a Categorizer that doesn't keep asking knows to fetch them." Then a row per Webhook, between hairlines like the tokens: the name (500), and under it a muted 12px line with the URL masked in monospace, as only its scheme, host and port ("https://ha.example:8123/••••", since the rest may hold the receiver's secret), then its latest attempt: "Delivered 27 Oct 2026 14:02", "Failed 27 Oct 2026 14:02: HTTP 500" (or "timed out", or "couldn't connect"; the failure in Ink 500), or "Never called". At the right are a 30px "Notify now" and a ghost 30px "Delete". Notify now calls that Webhook alone, even when nothing is waiting, and the row comes back with how it went; the button is disabled while it waits for the reply. A redirect counts as a failure ("HTTP 302"): it isn't followed. Delete confirms inline under the row: "Delete **Home Assistant**? Pursegraph stops calling it." (Keep it / Delete). There's no edit and no on/off switch: to change one, delete it and add it again. With no Webhook: "No Webhook yet, so a Categorizer finds waiting Transactions only by asking."
  - **New Webhook:** the reason line, with the info icon, says what is sent and where the secret goes: "It gets a POST of `{"event": "transactions_waiting", "at": …, "waiting": 3}`: how many are waiting, never what they are. Nothing signs it, so put the secret in the URL, which is shown masked once added." Then "Name" ("e.g. Home Assistant"), "URL" ("https://…", an http or https URL; a host with no domain, such as a Compose service, is fine) and the primary "Add Webhook" with the send icon.
- **Members.** The title row with the primary "Invite a Member", then a muted line: "Everyone in the Household who logs in. Admins also manage Members, Accounts, Categories and Categorizers. There is no email here: invite and reset links are passed on by hand."
  - **Grouped by role:** three cards, at most 900px wide: "Admins · 1" ("Manage Members, Accounts, Categories and Categorizers, and do everything a Member does."), "Members · 3" ("Import their Statements, work the Review queue and read the Dashboard.") and "Deactivated · 1" ("Can't log in. What they imported and decided stays."). The section a Member is in is their state, and the buttons on their row are the actions, so a state is never also a button.
  - **A row:** the avatar, then the name (500), with an Indigo Wash "You" tag on the current Member. Under it, a muted line gives the username and the Accounts they own, or "no Accounts of their own". A Member who hasn't used their invite gets a dashed "Hasn't set a password · invited 18 Oct" tag. At the right are 30px buttons: "Make admin" or "Make Member", "Reset link" (or "New invite link"), and a ghost "Deactivate". A deactivated Member has only "Reactivate". The last active admin has no Make Member or Deactivate, and the reason stands under their row.
  - **Confirms:** "Make Vera Novak an admin? Admins manage Members, Accounts, Categories and Categorizers, and can remove another admin's rights." (Cancel / Make admin). "Deactivate Luka Novak? Luka is logged out at once and can't log in. Their 2 Accounts, Statements and Transactions stay. You can reactivate them later." (Keep active / Deactivate). Make Member and Reactivate apply at once.
  - **Invite a Member:** a dialog with Name ("As the Dashboard shows it, e.g. in Per Member.") and Username ("What they log in with."), then "You'll get a one-time link to give them. They choose their own password through it. New Members aren't admins." and "Create and get the link".
  - **The link:** a dialog titled "Invite link for Ana" (or "Password-reset link for Luka"). It says who to give it to and what it does ("…They choose a new password through it, which also unlocks them if they were locked out."). Then the link in a read-only 40px monospace field with a primary "Copy link" (focused, turning to "✓ Copied"), and three facts with Indigo Ink icons: "**Shown only now.** Once you close this you can't see it again; make a new one from Members if it's lost.", "Works once, until **Tuesday 27 October 2026 at 14:05**." and "There is no email here. Pass it on yourself, in a message or in person." Done closes it.
